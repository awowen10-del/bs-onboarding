The Playbook · The First 42 Days
Hospitality is the sense that you’re on someone’s side.

That is the whole point of these 42 days. Every moment in this deck is built to feel personal, to anticipate rather than react, and to make a challenger feel seen. The mechanics are the easy part — what follows is why each one is worth doing properly.

Front-load the effort: the first two weeks decide whether someone stays.

Printing & posting

For the posted touchpoints, UK services that print & mail for you: ClickSend (no minimums, pay-as-you-go), Thanks.io (handwriting + one all-in price), or Scribeless (UK facility, handwriting styles). Any of them can eventually be triggered straight from this tracker once it’s on a shared backend.

Attendance

Bodysculpt PT sessions attended, week by week. Export the week's attendance from GoTeamUp and drop the file in — people are matched by email, and anyone in the file who isn't a member here is ignored. Three weeks each lower than the last, inside someone's first six months, puts a check-in on Today's moves.

One file a week. Re-uploading the same week replaces it rather than counting it twice, so it is safe to do again if you're not sure.

Add time to the journey

Pushed back a week or two? Add the days here and everything still ahead of them — texts, postcards, check-ins, the box — slides along together. Touchpoints already done stay put.

Follow up later

They're leaving on good terms — finished well, or having a go on their own. They count as Left for the conversion figure, exactly like the Left button. We'll just put them back on Today's moves on the date below so we actually reconnect.

Import dates of birth

Point this at a GoTeamUp export — Full Name, First Name, Last Name, Email, Date of Birth. Rows are matched to challengers already in the tracker by name (email breaks a tie) and their date of birth is filled in. Nobody is ever created: a file of 300 members will only touch the handful who are in here, and everyone else is skipped.
